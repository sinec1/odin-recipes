Horchata

Description

A sweet drink made from blending white rice

Ingredients

  • Water
  • Rice
  • Sugar
  • Milk
  • Optional flavorings (Vanilla extract, Cinnamon)

Steps

  1. Blend rice and water together for about 1 minute
  2. Rest 3 hours
  3. Strain rice water into a pitcher
  4. Stir sugar, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon in
  5. Chill and serve with ice
