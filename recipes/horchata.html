Horchata

Description

Ingredients

  1. Water
  2. Rice
  3. Sugar
  4. Milk
  5. Optional flavorings (Vanilla extract, Cinnamon)

Instructions

  1. Blend rice and water together for about 1 minute
  2. Rest 3 hours
  3. Strain rice water into a pitcher
  4. Stir sugar, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon in
  5. Chill and serve with ice
