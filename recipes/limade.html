Agua Fresca de Pepino (Cucumber Limade)

Description

Cucumber agua fresca, typically served over ice

Ingredients

  • Water
  • Cucumbers
  • Fresh lime juice
  • Sugar/Sucralose sweetener

Steps

  1. Blend water, cucumbers, lime juice, and sweetener of choice until smooth
  2. Pour into pitcher and add more water and sweetener as needed
  3. Serve over ice
