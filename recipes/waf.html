Watermelon Agua Fresca

Description

Cold and refreshing dink great for a hot day

Ingredients

  • Cubed watermelon
  • Water
  • White sugar
  • Lime
  • Mint leaves
  • Ice (to serve)

Steps

  1. Blend watermelon and water until smooth, add sugar to taste
  2. To serve, place half a lime slice into a glass with some mint leaves
  3. Crush mint and lime, add ice and blended watermelon, mix well and enjoy
